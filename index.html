Who I am

Building with purpose

I am a final-year Diploma in Multimedia Computing student at Tshwane University of Technology, Pretoria. My work sits at the intersection of design, development and digital media — I care deeply about creating things that are functional, well-crafted and genuinely useful to people.

I built QueryCrest, a live student platform helping applicants navigate the university application process — designed, developed and deployed entirely on my own. That project showed me what it takes to ship something real.

I hold a Cisco Cybersecurity certificate, which reflects a growing curiosity in how networks operate, where vulnerabilities exist, and how digital systems can be made more secure.

I am actively seeking a Work Integrated Learning (WIL) placement where I can grow beyond what the classroom offers, contribute to real projects, and learn from experienced professionals.
